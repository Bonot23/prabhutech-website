About Prabhu Tech Ltd

Who We Are

Prabhu Tech Ltd is a UK-registered business supplying high-quality electronic components and hands-on engineering support to students, universities, and companies across the United Kingdom. We support electronics engineering labs, robotics clubs, and academic departments by providing reliable hardware and technical guidance. To make purchasing simple for academic institutions, we accept official university Purchase Orders (PO).

Our Educational & Project Support

We don't just supply parts—we actively help build the next generation of engineers. Our team offers dedicated technical support to help students and researchers turn their ideas into working hardware:

  • Student Project Support: We guide and assist students in troubleshooting, selecting components, and successfully building their academic and personal electronics projects.
  • Practical Training: We teach practical, hands-on concepts in Electronics, Robotics, and PCB (Printed Circuit Board) Design.

Assembly & Repair Services

Whether you need custom prototypes assembled or faulty laboratory equipment restored, we offer reliable hardware services:

  • PCB Assembly & Soldering: High-quality assembly services for both modern Surface-Mount Technology (SMD) and classic Through-Hole Technology (THT) components.
  • Board-Level Repair: Fast diagnostics, trace repair, micro-soldering, and component replacement to bring faulty or damaged PCBs back to life.
